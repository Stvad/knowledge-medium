Anatomy of a block

Packing list
Places to eat
  1. 1Bullet. Click it to zoom in, so the block fills the panel. Drag it to move the block. Right-click it for the block's menu. (On touch, hold it, then drag or let go.)
  2. 2Chevron. Appears on hover when a block has children. Click to fold or unfold them.
  3. 3Text. Click to edit. Markdown works: bold, lists, tables, code.
  4. 4Link. [[Lisbon]] links to the page named Lisbon, and creates it if it doesn't exist.
  5. 5Type. #project gives the block a type. Some types add behaviour, like a checkbox.
  6. 6References. How many other blocks link here. Click to list them under the block.
  7. 7Comments. Open comment threads on the block. Click to read and reply.
  8. 8Ring. A hollow bullet marks a page. Pages nest like any other block.
  9. 9Halo. A folded block whose children are hidden.
